HOW IT WORKS

Contact the business and explain what you would like to donate. The current site says donated cars can be supplied to the Western Australia Fire Department for training and practising road-crash rescue techniques.

This redesigned site also includes an enquiry route for usable parts. Parts donations should be treated as an enquiry rather than a guaranteed service, so the team can confirm what is suitable.

Donation noteThe Fire Department donation information is based on the existing Scrap Car Perth website. Parts donation is presented here as an enquiry option for the redesign.
