SERVICES

Practical vehicle solutions, from removal to recycling.

Scrap Car Perth's published service offering covers scrap car removal, cash for cars, car recycling and auto wrecking in Perth.

01 • SCRAP CAR REMOVAL

Get the vehicle out of the driveway without the hassle.

The business says it removes scrap, unwanted, unused and accident-damaged cars across Perth. Towing is arranged so customers do not have to drive an unusable vehicle to a yard.

  • Perth and surrounding suburbs
  • Cars in a range of conditions
  • Removal after purchase is agreed
  • Paperwork and logistics handled as part of the process
02 • CASH FOR CARS

Know what your car is worth before you let it go.

Quotes depend on factors such as the vehicle's condition, make, model and circumstances. The published site says a free vehicle evaluation can be scheduled when more information is needed.

  • Free quote request
  • Evaluation available for complex vehicles
  • Old, unwanted, damaged or working cars
  • Instant quote after evaluation
03 • CAR RECYCLING

Recover useful parts. Sort the rest responsibly.

The current site explains that cars are dismantled into components such as wheels, lights, engines, seats, transmissions, exhausts, mirrors, batteries and other parts. Reusable components can be separated from scrap materials for appropriate recycling or disposal.

  • Reusable components separated
  • Scrap materials sorted for recycling
  • Vehicles handled after collection
  • Supports a practical end-of-life pathway
04 • AUTO WRECKERS

Vehicles of many makes, years and conditions.

The business says it buys from individuals, private sellers, auctions, mining companies, mechanics, towing businesses, dealers, government agencies and other sources.
